# Quant research demo design

The public demo presents one research loop: find credible papers, turn an idea into a rule, then judge it only on later data after costs.

## Paper library

The landing page behaves like a reading room. Search, topics, citations, saving, Crossref collection, import and BibTeX/JSON export all use the real catalogue model. The forward-test strip keeps the next stage visible without turning the page into a trading dashboard.

## Walk-forward workbench

The detailed experiment uses the bundled SPY history and the real walk-forward model. Inputs sit beside the output because training length, test length and costs are the decisions that materially change the result. The equity chart, frozen decisions table, exports and 50-strategy archive remain available without crowding the paper finder.

## Visual language

- Paper pages use editorial serif headlines and restrained blue-grey ink.
- Backtests use a compact research-terminal layout with amber for the tested strategy and blue for the benchmark.
- Dense information must remain readable at 100% zoom and on mobile.
- No decorative gradients, generic card grids or unsupported performance claims.
