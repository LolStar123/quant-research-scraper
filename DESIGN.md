# Quant research: a working research index

## Direction, before implementation

A researcher collects papers and tests a market rule. Finding and saving a paper is the library's primary action; an adjacent, real SPY experiment makes the next stage tangible. Papers do not implement themselves. Keep the browser moving-average experiment separate from the fifty-strategy archive. Name the opening view Paper library and link directly to the separate archive.

References inspected: the locally archived Linear render and DESIGN.md (2 October 2026) supply quiet layered surfaces, aligned controls and restrained typography. The existing citation index supplies the paper hierarchy. The root's inspected Observable Plot reference supplies readable axes and comparison marks. Borrow principles, not a brand, marketing hero or copy.

The signature is a citation index beside an inspectable historical experiment. Replace the oversized pipeline slogan and unlabelled miniature plot. On phones, paper results and reading-list controls precede the experiment; the chart is rebuilt at its rendered width.

## States and layout

Saved/topic filters expose aria-pressed; active workflow links expose aria-current. Empty search gives a usable next action. Disabled pagination/export stays visible. Controls, links, chart and import have visible focus and 40px hit areas. Local-storage failure asks for export. Remote failure retains the bundled library. Experiment actions are disabled until prices load.

Pointer/touch and ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/Home/End inspect both equity values on one date. Resizing redraws at actual pixel scale without recalculating returns. The detailed workbench retains decisions and CSV export. Separate archive notes identify selection bias and unresolved corporate-action adjustment provenance.

Below 900px the desk becomes one column; topics wrap, controls reflow and tables scroll inside their container. No body overflow, entrance sequence or decorative assets. Reduced motion disables short button transitions.

Inspect 1280px and 390px screenshots, browser errors and unchanged model/data hashes. QA is ignored under output/redesign; accepted README screenshots are versioned deliberately.
